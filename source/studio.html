Physical color palette

Read colors, edit locally, then apply. Every successful write is checked against the device.

The SMC-PAD stores eight presets, and only the one it is currently using lights the pads. If Apply reports success but the pads do not change, choose Identify: the pads flash white for a moment only when the selected preset and bank are the ones on the hardware. Try each preset until they flash.
