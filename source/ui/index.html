Physical color palette

Read colors, edit locally, then apply. Every successful write is checked against the device.

Studio reads the active preset and PAD BANK switch directly from the device. Apply palette updates that preset; the hardware never switches presets during a color write. PAD BANK off is Bank A, and PAD BANK on is Bank B. Choose Identify to flash the selected bank white and restore its colors.

Behavior & accessibility

Studio connects at startup and reconnects after the USB cable is unplugged. Disconnect on the Device page pauses this until you connect again.

Pinned profiles always take precedence. App rules are set per profile.

Uses the preset the controller is on right now. Each pad returns to its stored color when playback stops. Temporary colors are never saved to the device.

Background behavior

Closing the window hides Studio to the tray. Native MIDI and audio continue. Pause mappings or stop audio any time from the tray menu.
